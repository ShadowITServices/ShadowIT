## Why this site exists

In the industry, "Shadow IT" means the tools running outside formal oversight —
the hidden layer most organisations only notice when something breaks.

This site turns that around: a public record of what I've learned across
Microsoft 365, cybersecurity, and IT professionalism. Not a sales pitch — the
reasoning, not just the result.

## Background

## How I work

Everything here is grounded in the [ACS Code of Professional Conduct](https://www.acs.org.au/content/dam/acs/rules-and-regulations/Code-of-Professional-Conduct_v2.1.pdf),
whose highest value is the **Primacy of the Public Interest**. In practice:

- **Security that protects people.** A few well-chosen protections over who can
  log in, the devices they use, and where the data lives — matched to real risk,
  not the longest tool list.
- **Write things down.** Competent work can be checked and handed over. The
  guides and runbooks here come from that habit.
- **Plain language, no surprises.** "Managing the unseen" only works if the
  people relying on you can see that it's handled.
