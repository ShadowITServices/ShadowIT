## Why Shadow IT exists

> Shadow IT was born out of my pursuit for better outcomes and IT professional
> standards. Instead of pursuing my own business, I decided to create a blog and
> public repository of my body of knowledge across Microsoft 365, Cybersecurity,
> and IT Professionalism.

The name is a deliberate play on the term. "Shadow IT" usually describes the
tools and systems that operate outside formal oversight — the unseen layer most
organisations only notice when something breaks. This site is my attempt to drag
that layer into the light: to document what good looks like, to hold my own work
to a professional standard, and to share the reasoning, not just the result.

It isn't a sales pitch. It's a public, evolving record of how I think about and
practise IT.

## How I approach IT

**Security first, but proportionate.** Good security for a small or mid-sized
organisation isn't about buying the most products; it's about layered,
maintainable controls over identity, endpoints, and data that match the real
risk. I'd rather harden the basics well than chase complexity that won't be kept
up.

**Governance you can stand behind.** Configuration, access, and change should be
documented and defensible — not because an auditor is watching, but because it's
how you keep a system understandable a year from now. The runbooks and guides on
this site reflect that bias toward writing things down.

**Communication without the jargon.** The fastest way to lose a client's trust
is to make them feel out of the loop. I try to explain the *why* in plain terms,
surface the work happening behind the scenes, and avoid surprises. "Managing the
unseen" only works if the people relying on you can see that it's being managed.
